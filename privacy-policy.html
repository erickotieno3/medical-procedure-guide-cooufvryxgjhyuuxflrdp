Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

We do NOT collect any personal data. No name, email, location, or health information is collected. The only data handled is the Advertising ID (via Google AdMob) and anonymous crash logs.

1. Information We Collect

This app does not collect or store personal information. We do NOT collect:

  • Name or email address
  • Location data
  • Health or medical information
  • Financial information
  • Photos, contacts, or messages

2. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

This app uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob automatically collects the Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier) for ad targeting. This data is handled by AdMob — not by us directly.

Opt out: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID

See Google Privacy Policy.

Crash Reporting

Anonymous crash logs are collected to improve app stability. No personal data is included and logs are not shared with third parties.

3. Affiliate Links

This app contains affiliate links. Clicking one may allow the partner site to track the referral. We earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We do not share personal data via affiliate links.

4. Data Security

Data is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS/TLS.

5. Data Deletion

We do not store personal data, so there is nothing to delete on our end. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. To reset AdMob Advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

Updates to this policy will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
