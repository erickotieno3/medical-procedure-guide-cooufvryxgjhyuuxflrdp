Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 13, 2026

Medical Procedure Guide (“the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting user information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when using the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require user registration and does not request or store personal medical records.

However, limited non-personal information may be automatically collected through third-party services integrated into the App, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may collect and process certain device identifiers, usage data, and advertising-related information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Learn more about Google’s privacy practices here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Affiliate Links

The App may contain affiliate links to third-party websites, products, or services. If users click on affiliate links, the developer may earn a commission at no additional cost to the user.

Third-party websites may collect information according to their own privacy policies. Users are encouraged to review those policies before interacting with external services.

4. Data Safety and Security

Information transmitted through supported third-party services is encrypted in transit where applicable.

The App does not intentionally collect or store:

  • Personal health records
  • Passwords
  • Government identification numbers
  • Financial account information
  • Precise location data

The App is designed for informational and educational purposes only and should not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 years and older. The App is not directed toward children under 13 years of age, and no knowingly collected children's data is stored by the App.

6. Data Deletion

Since the App does not provide account registration or direct user profile storage, users may stop all data collection by uninstalling the App from their device.

Some third-party services such as Google AdMob may independently process certain device identifiers according to their own policies.

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in the App, legal requirements, or third-party services. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
